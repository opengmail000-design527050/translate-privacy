Translate 隐私权政策

适用于 Chrome 扩展 Translate（网页、YouTube 字幕和直播的双语翻译）· 生效日期：2026 年 10 月 19 日

一句话

这个扩展没有自己的服务器，不收集、不出售任何数据，也没有统计和广告。你要翻译的内容只会发给你自己在设置页填的接口 （比如 OpenAI、Anthropic，或者你自建的服务），用来翻译或识别语音；其余东西都只存在你这台电脑的浏览器里。

会发出去的东西，发给谁

以下内容只在你打开相应的翻译时，发给你配置的接口地址（扩展的开发者收不到）：

  • 网页翻译：页面上要翻译的段落文字（默认只翻出现在屏幕上的段落），以及页面标题（帮助模型判断领域）。 只有你在弹窗里打开翻译、按快捷键，或者把这个网站设成「总是翻译」时才会发生。
  • YouTube 字幕：视频的字幕文字，以及视频标题、简介里的一段（帮助统一人名和术语）。
  • 直播和回放字幕：视频里有字幕时，发字幕文字。没有字幕、或者你在弹窗里点了「改用语音识别」时， 录下这个标签页的声音发给你配置的语音识别接口，再把识别出的文字发给翻译接口；录音需要你在弹窗里另外授权。
  • 回放字幕中断时补识别：Twitch / Kick 回放用的是视频里主播自己的字幕；主播的字幕软件中途停掉、 而那一段有人声时，扩展会从视频数据里取出那一段的声音（不是录标签页），发给你配置的语音识别接口补上。 只有你配置了语音识别接口才会这样做。
  • 你的 API Key：只作为请求头，发给你为它填写的那个接口地址。

这些接口由你选择，它们怎样处理收到的内容，以各自的隐私权政策为准。

读取视频里的字幕

  • Twitch 回放：你授权 www.twitch.tv 之后，扩展在 Twitch 页面里读取播放器本来就在下载的视频数据， 从中取出主播嵌在视频里的字幕。不另外下载，不调用 Twitch 的接口；读到的数据只留在页面里，你打开直播翻译时才拿来翻译。
  • Kick 回放：你打开直播翻译时，扩展从 Kick 的视频服务器下载播放位置附近最低清晰度的几小段视频，读里面的字幕 （字幕中断时也取其中那一段的声音补识别，见上），请求不带 cookie。
  • YouTube：字幕从 YouTube 自己的字幕接口读取，跟播放器显示字幕时取的是同一份。

只存在你电脑上的东西

  • 设置、接口配置和 API Key：存在浏览器的扩展存储里（chrome.storage.local），不同步到其他设备。 网页里运行的脚本读不到 Key。
  • 译文缓存和用量统计：存在本机，重看时不用再花钱；可以在设置页一键清空，卸载扩展时一起删除。
  • 诊断信息：只在你点「复制诊断信息」或「导出诊断」时生成，进你自己的剪贴板或下载文件夹；不含 API Key。
  • 导出设置：你点「导出设置」得到的文件里有 API Key（明文），请自己保管。

权限

每项权限的用途写在扩展的商店页上。网页翻译、录音（标签页声音）、Twitch、自定义接口地址都是用到时才向你申请，你可以随时在 Chrome 的扩展设置里收回。

变更与联系

Translate Privacy Policy

Applies to the Chrome extension Translate (bilingual translation for web pages, YouTube subtitles and live streams) · Effective October 19, 2026

In short

The extension has no server of its own, collects and sells nothing, and has no analytics or ads. The content you choose to translate is sent only to the endpoint you configure in its settings (for example OpenAI, Anthropic, or your own service) for translation or speech recognition. Everything else stays in this browser on your computer.

What is sent, and to whom

Only when you turn on the corresponding translation, and only to the endpoint you configured (never to the developer):

  • Web pages: the paragraphs being translated (by default only those on screen) and the page title as a topic hint — when you turn translation on in the popup, press the shortcut, or have marked the site “always translate”.
  • YouTube subtitles: the subtitle text, plus the video title and part of its description to keep names and terms consistent.
  • Live streams and replays: the video's own captions when it has them. When it has none, or when you click “use speech recognition” in the popup, the tab's audio is captured and sent to the speech-recognition endpoint you configured, and the recognized text to the translation endpoint; capturing tab audio needs a separate permission you grant in the popup.
  • Filling caption gaps on replays: Twitch and Kick replays use the streamer's own embedded captions. When the streamer's caption software stops for a while and someone is speaking, the extension takes that stretch of audio from the video data (not by recording the tab) and sends it to your speech-recognition endpoint to fill the gap — only if you have configured one.
  • Your API key: sent only as a request header to the endpoint you entered it for.

You choose these endpoints; their own privacy policies govern what they do with what they receive.

Reading captions embedded in video

  • Twitch replays: after you grant www.twitch.tv, the extension reads, inside the Twitch page, the video data the player is already downloading, and extracts the captions the streamer embedded in it. Nothing extra is downloaded and no Twitch API is called; the data stays in the page and is used only while you have live translation on.
  • Kick replays: when you turn on live translation, the extension downloads a few short lowest-quality segments around the playhead from Kick's video servers, without cookies, to read the captions in them (and, for caption gaps, that stretch of audio; see above).
  • YouTube: subtitles come from YouTube's own caption endpoint, the same data the player shows.

What stays on your computer

  • Settings, endpoint profiles and API keys, in the extension's storage (chrome.storage.local), not synced. Scripts running in web pages cannot read your keys.
  • The translation cache and usage counters, local only; clear them in settings, and they are removed when you uninstall.
  • Diagnostics, created only when you click “copy diagnostics” or “export diagnostics”, go to your clipboard or downloads folder and never contain your API key.
  • Exported settings files contain your API keys in plain text; keep them safe.
